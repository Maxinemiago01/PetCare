# Petcare design direction

The user's brief: a slightly cartoony app for pet lovers, like a digital notebook, using the provided pet photos and paw-print image.

Reading this as a warm, approachable pet notebook for pet owners. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Cream pages and cocoa text borrow warmth from the supplied brown paw illustration. Terracotta highlights the main action; muted sage is reserved for health and care status.
- Nunito's rounded letterforms make forms and headings friendly while remaining readable.
- The user's paw image is the brand motif. The supplied cat-and-dog portrait belongs on authentication screens, establishing that this notebook welcomes different pets.
- A sidebar keeps desktop navigation stable; a compact bottom navigation keeps the two primary destinations reachable on a phone.
- Pet cards use consistent sizing for scanning real profiles. Notes, record lists, and reminder rows use their own content-driven layouts.
- Inputs have a 12px radius, buttons 14px, notebook panels 22px. Photos use a softer crop to distinguish them from controls.
- Borders keep the notebook flat. Only dialogs use shadows to show elevation.
- Icon meanings: paw for pets, calendar for care dates, heart for health, medical for vet visits, camera for photo selection.
- The auth submit arrow indicates progressing into the notebook; chevrons identify links that open pet details or reminders. Species tags identify pets, and care-status labels distinguish overdue and upcoming dates.
- Motion is limited to interaction feedback, loading indicators, and Ionic dialogs. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Empty, loading, error, and missing-photo states are part of the app. No fictional pets or records are presented as user data.
